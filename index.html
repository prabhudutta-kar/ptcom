Pooja Tibrewal is a Wellness, Mindfulness, and Life Coach with over 12 years of experience in helping individuals and organizations cultivate balance, clarity and purpose in their everyday lives.

She is a seasoned finance professional and an
alumna of IIM Ranchi.

She specializes in enhancing workplace wellbeing and team dynamics through experiential programs that integrate stress management, mindful communication, meaningful relationships and collaborative leadership.

Journey with

The Art of Living Foundation

On the other side of her work lies her passion for human transformation and well-being. Her journey began as a
Faculty with The Art of Living Foundation, one of the world’s leading NGOs focused on humanitarian work spread over more than 180+ countries.

She has trained thousands of professionals, educators, and students across India on fostering well-being through breath, meditation and yoga programs.

Over the years, she has witnessed that true change begins when individuals reconnect with themselves through a healthier lifestyle, awareness and action.
